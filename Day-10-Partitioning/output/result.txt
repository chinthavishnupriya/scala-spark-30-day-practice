--- When to Increase Partitions ---
Increase partitions when:
  The dataset is large.
  Existing partitions contain too much data.
  More parallelism is available.
  Tasks are taking too long.

--- When to Decrease Partitions ---
Decrease partitions when:
  There are too many small partitions.
  Task scheduling overhead becomes significant.
  A smaller number of output files is desired.

--- Optimization Scenario ---
Problem:
  The original dataset has only 1 partition.
Optimization:
  Repartition the dataset to 4 partitions.
Reason:
  More partitions allow more tasks to process data
  concurrently when multiple CPU cores are available.
